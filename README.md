# Aviation Accident Analysis
After cleaning the dataset I frirst droped null values in the final_cleaned_aviation_flight_data.csv but during EDA it created a bit of a challenge because columns for extra visualizations were removed. hence we had to keep them.

The required was to keep columns with more than 20000 non nulls however the largest after cleaning had 17892 hence we opted to work with 16000 non null columns.

We discovered for the insuarance to up the profit they have to increase the fee on the large aircrafts in general since they have alot of factors that makes them costly when an accident occurs hence surge in rate on serious/fatal injuries, number  of fatal injuries and the extent of destruction to the aircraft.

Factors such as weather condition and number of engines directly influence the degree of damage to the aircraft when an accident occurs and the number of injuries recorded.

The outlier is the small aircrafts since they had the highest records in sampled data.
